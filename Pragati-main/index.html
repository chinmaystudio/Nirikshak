Nirikshak is a digital infrastructure monitoring platform that connects government authorities, contractors, engineers, and citizens through a single evidence-driven project lifecycle.

From tender allocation to on-ground execution and final completion, Nirikshak brings project data, financial records, field evidence, AI insights, and citizen feedback into one transparent system.

01 / Connected Governance

One Project. Every Stakeholder.
One Source of Truth.

Public infrastructure involves multiple departments, agencies, contractors, engineers, and citizens. Nirikshak brings them together around the same project data.

  • 🏛️
    Government Authorities Monitor project progress, approvals, budgets, milestones, and contractor performance from a unified dashboard.
  • 🏗️
    Contractors & Engineers Submit work updates, documents, measurements, bills, photographs, and site evidence directly from the field.
  • 👥
    Citizens Discover nearby projects, track their progress, understand how public funds are being used, and raise issues when work doesn't match expectations.

03 / Evidence-Based Monitoring

Know What Is Happening
on the Ground.

Project dashboards shouldn't depend only on manually reported progress. Nirikshak combines multiple sources of evidence to help authorities understand the actual status of a project.

  • 📍
    Geo-Tagged Field Evidence Capture photographs, videos, location, timestamps, and site observations from project locations.
  • 🛰️
    Drone & Visual Analysis Use aerial imagery and computer vision to support physical progress verification where applicable.
  • 📊
    Progress Intelligence Compare planned milestones with reported progress, expenditure, and available field evidence.
✦ AI highlights inconsistencies and potential risks — while final decisions remain with authorized officials.

Turn Project Data

Finance

Resources

  • ₹
    Project Budget Understand the sanctioned and revised project cost.
  • %
    Financial Progress Compare expenditure with overall project progress.
  • ✓

07 / AI-Powered Insights

AI Finds the Signals.
Officers Make the Decisions.

Nirikshak uses AI to assist infrastructure governance — not replace it.

  • 🔍
    Anomaly Detection Identify unusual patterns in project progress, expenditure, or reporting.
  • ⏱️
    Delay Prediction Use historical and project data to identify projects that may be at risk of missing milestones.
  • 📑
    Document Intelligence Extract and organize relevant information from tenders, contracts, reports, and project documents.
  • ⚖️
    Dispute Support Bring together timelines, submissions, site evidence, and project records to support transparent review.
✦ AI provides signals and evidence. Accountability stays with the authorized decision-maker.
