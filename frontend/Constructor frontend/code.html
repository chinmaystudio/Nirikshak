Public Accountability Architecture

How Citizen Surveillance Works

Four simple stages bridging citizen reporting with government engineers

01. Track Project

Locate public works in your ward, verify sanctioned budgets, contractor details, and committed completion deadlines.

02. Capture Evidence

Notice potholes, substandard materials, or unannounced road closures? Upload geotagged photos with timestamp verification.

03. Automated Notice

The portal generates a legal tracking token and automatically routes the inquiry to the designated Executive Engineer.

04. Redressal & Audit

Contractor is penalized or rectified under public audit scrutiny. Status updates are published transparently on your dashboard.
