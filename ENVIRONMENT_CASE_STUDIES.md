# NIRIKSHAK PLATFORM — ENVIRONMENTAL VALIDATION & CASE STUDIES
**Focus**: Ground Truth vs. Statutory Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Commitments  
**Rule**: AI outputs "Potential deviation" rather than "Confirmed violation" until official Government verification.

---

## Case Study 1: Urban Dust Suppression & Air Quality
- **Project**: Pune Metro Line 3 — Shivajinagar Interchange Segment
- **EIA Statutory Commitment**: Continuous water sprinkling on unpaved service roads; particulate matter ($PM_{10}$) mitigation.
- **Multi-Source Evidence**:
  - *Contractor Filing*: Claims twice-daily water bowser operation.
  - *Citizen Complaints*: 6 geolocated complaints within 48 hours reporting severe airborne dust near bus stops.
  - *External Signal*: Regional Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) sensor nearby reporting $PM_{10} > 280\ \mu\text{g/m}^3$.
- **Nemotron AI Risk Interpretation**:
  - *Finding*: *"Potential dust suppression deviation flagged. Discrepancy observed between reported bowser logs and clustered citizen air-quality complaints."*
  - *Risk Level*: `MEDIUM`
- **Government Response**:
  - Executive Engineer conducts unannounced site audit, verifies inadequate bowser frequency, issues formal rectification notice, and approves corrective water sprinkling schedule.

## Case Study 2: Monsoon Waterlogging & Drainage Obstruction
- **Project**: Pune Ring Road — Package III
- **EIA Statutory Commitment**: Preservation of natural culvert discharge paths; zero impoundment of surface runoff.
- **Multi-Source Evidence**:
  - *Contractor Filing*: Substructure embankment completed.
  - *Citizen Complaints*: 4 complaints from adjacent farmers showing photographic evidence of inundated agricultural plots.
  - *Drone Footage*: Identifies temporary earth berm blocking 1,200 mm hume-pipe cross drainage.
- **Nemotron AI Risk Interpretation**:
  - *Finding*: *"Hydrological anomaly detected. Evidence indicates potential cross-drainage culvert obstruction during active monsoon window."*
  - *Risk Level*: `HIGH`
- **Government Response**:
  - Joint inspection order issued within 4 hours; contractor ordered to immediately excavate bypass trench and desilt culvert barrel.

## Case Study 3: Construction Debris & Demolition Waste Disposal
- **Project**: Mula-Mutha River Rejuvenation
- **EIA Statutory Commitment**: Zero dumping within 100 meters of the high-flood line (HFL).
- **Multi-Source Evidence**:
  - *Citizen Grievance*: Geo-tagged photo of concrete debris dumped on riparian buffer zone.
  - *Contractor Report*: Waste manifest shows off-site transport to authorized processing facility.
- **Nemotron AI Risk Interpretation**:
  - *Finding*: *"Potential riparian buffer encroachment. Geo-coordinates of citizen evidence conflict with off-site disposal manifest."*
  - *Risk Level*: `HIGH`
- **Government Response**:
  - Strict penalty notice issued to primary contractor; removal of 45 tonnes of dumped rubble enforced and verified with geo-tagged photographic evidence.
